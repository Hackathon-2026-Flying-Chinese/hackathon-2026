How Viva solves it

Four steps. Inside every pull request.

1 · Triage

Score every PR

Risk × novelty × your proven level. Routine work passes untouched.

2 · Three-minute viva

Explain your change

A short AI interview on risky or new work: how it works and why, in your own words. Graded on what you say, never face, voice or accent.

3 · Only real gaps

Seniors see clips

A first miss is feedback and new questions. Repeat misses, or very high risk, go to a senior as timestamped clips.

4 · Earn trust

Each pass gets lighter

Your level on the concept rises, so the next similar change gets a lighter check.
