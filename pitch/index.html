How Viva solves it

Four steps. Inside every pull request.

1 · Triage

Score every PR

Risk × novelty × your proven level. Routine work passes untouched.

Explain your change

Where it plugs in

GitHub AppA required check with a status and a link. Never answers or media.

Slack AppReminders, and the review queue for seniors.

Here's the full design. The Viva workspace runs in the browser: the interview, voice and video, the reveal and senior review. The backend is Python, FastAPI on Uvicorn, with SQLite and one encryption key per tenant. Claude Sonnet 5.5 asks one follow-up and marks against a fixed rubric, or you run Bedrock or an open-weight model in your own cloud.

It plugs into the tools teams already use: a GitHub App that adds a required check, a job on your own CI runner that takes only the diff and runs the checks, a Slack App for reminders, and speech-to-text inside your environment.

[If asked what runs today: the demo is the workspace plus a small Python server calling Claude; the FastAPI and SQLite learning-loop backend is a separate prototype with 14 tests. GitHub, CI runner, Slack and Whisper are next.]
